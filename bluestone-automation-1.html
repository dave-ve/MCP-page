Bluestone PIM

Automation capabilities

The headless, agent-first product data platform. Automation runs through the interface, your own APIs, or autonomous agents, so the work happens without anyone triggering it each time.

Type of automation When you'd want it What runs automatically What your team gets How you control it

Rule‑Based Automation

Deterministic
When you'd want it

You're repeating the same manual checks and steps, and need catalogue data to stay consistent and error‑free as volume grows.

What runs automatically
  • Incoming products are categorised, validated and released on your own business logic
  • Duplicates and bad data are blocked at entry, and every change is checked the moment it happens, with full lineage logged
What your team gets

Consistent, publish‑ready data with no manual policing, and a clear record of who or what changed what.

How you control it

You define the logic once; it runs the same whether driven from the interface or your own APIs.

AI‑Powered Automation

AI in the rules
When you'd want it

Enrichment (descriptions, SEO, translations) is your bottleneck, and you're expanding into more products, markets or languages.

What runs automatically
  • Continuous AI enrichment, translation and image tagging across the catalogue
  • Triggered by events rather than a button, with no one generating content product by product
What your team gets

Enrich and localise in hours, not weeks, so your team covers far more products.

How you control it

Human sign‑off is optional: require approval before it publishes, or let it run. Bring your own AI model.

Autonomous Agents

Agentic
When you'd want it

The work is open‑ended and multi‑step. You set the goal and guardrails and the agent works out the steps, rather than you scripting a rule for each one.

What runs automatically
  • Agents plan and run multi‑step jobs end to end (supplier onboarding, validation, syndication) unattended on a trigger
  • Or just ask in plain language: find, edit, analyse and bulk‑update across your catalogue
  • And your data is structured so external AI, such as ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity, can find and act on it too
What your team gets

Scale the catalogue without scaling headcount, as an agent does as much as one of your developers could.

How you control it

Agents act within your rules and are checked and logged like any user, recording who or what made each change.
